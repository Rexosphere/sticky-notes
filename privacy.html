Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 3, 2026  |  Developer: Rexosphere

Sticky Notes ("the App") is developed and maintained by Rexosphere. This Privacy Policy explains how our application handles your information.

1. Information Collection and Storage

Sticky Notes is an offline-first productivity application:

  • No Personal Data Collected: The App does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal information, device identifiers, or usage analytics.
  • Local Storage: All sticky notes, tasks, and preferences you create are stored exclusively on your local device using secure local storage.

2. Permissions & Third-Party Services

  • The App does not require or request access to your contacts, location, camera, or microphone.
  • The App does not include third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.

3. Data Retention and Deletion

Since all data is stored exclusively on your device, deleting the application or clearing the application data through your Android settings will permanently delete all stored notes and settings.
